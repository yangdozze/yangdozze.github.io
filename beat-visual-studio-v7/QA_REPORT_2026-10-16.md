## Outstanding QA / limitations
- Browser tool captured screenshots during runs, but no standalone downloadable full-page 390px / 320px PNG screenshot artifact was provided. Full-page image QA therefore remains pending.
- Full pixel-by-pixel animation comparison with original METHOD has not been validated: reference's audio preview required uploading a track, unavailable in the reference QA run.
- The A/B confirms signal-dependent pixels, not guaranteed visual quality or smooth 60fps on iPhone Safari.
- WebCodecs MP4 support varies by browser; the verified output here was **WebM**, not MP4. 60fps/1080p, long 180s jobs, cancellation/recovery, and audio sync on real beats remain to be verified.
- The existing MediaRecorder fallback is realtime and **may drop frames** under heavy load; deterministic path is the recommended supported mode.
- Validate fullscreen on real iPhone and Android with media loaded, and test authentic Capri/Umina/Mozgi/UK UK/Bristles/Too Soon in v7 browser.
